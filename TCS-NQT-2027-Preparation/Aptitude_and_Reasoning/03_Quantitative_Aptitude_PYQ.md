# Quantitative Aptitude - TCS NQT Last 5 Years PYQs

The Quantitative section in TCS NQT is known to be lengthy. Here are the most repeated models of questions from the last 5 years.

---

## 1. Number System (Remainders)
**Question:** What is the remainder when $17^{200}$ is divided by 18?
**Solution Framework:**
Use the binomial expansion concept or the negative remainder concept.
- $17 \equiv -1 \pmod{18}$
- Therefore, $17^{200} \equiv (-1)^{200} \pmod{18}$
- Since the power is even, $(-1)^{200} = 1$.
**Answer: 1**

## 2. Time and Work (Alternating Days)
**Question:** A can do a piece of work in 10 days and B can do it in 15 days. If they work on alternate days starting with A, in how many days will the work be completed?
**Solution Framework:**
1. Let Total Work = LCM(10, 15) = 30 units.
2. Efficiency of A = 3 units/day. Efficiency of B = 2 units/day.
3. In a 2-day cycle: A works day 1 (3 units), B works day 2 (2 units).
4. Work done in 2 days = $3 + 2 = 5$ units.
5. Number of such cycles to complete 30 units = $30 / 5 = 6$ cycles.
6. Total days = $6 \times 2 = 12$ days.
**Answer: 12 days**

## 3. Time, Speed, and Distance (Trains)
**Question:** A train 150m long is running at a speed of 90 km/hr. How much time will it take to cross a platform 250m long?
**Solution Framework:**
1. Total Distance to cross = Length of Train + Length of Platform = $150 + 250 = 400m$.
2. Speed of train = $90 \text{ km/hr}$. Convert to m/s: $90 \times (5/18) = 25 \text{ m/s}$.
3. Time = Distance / Speed = $400 / 25 = 16$ seconds.
**Answer: 16 seconds**

## 4. Profit, Loss, and Discount (Dishonest Dealer)
**Question:** A dishonest dealer marks up his goods by 20% and then gives a discount of 10%. He also uses a 900gm weight instead of 1kg. Find his overall profit percentage.
**Solution Framework:**
Use successive percentage changes or the ratio method.
Let CP of 1000g = 1000 Rs.
1. Weight given is 900g. Actual Cost Price for him = 900 Rs.
2. He marks up 1000g by 20% -> 1200 Rs.
3. He gives a 10% discount on 1200 Rs -> Selling Price = $1200 - 120 = 1080$ Rs.
4. Profit = SP - CP = $1080 - 900 = 180$ Rs.
5. Profit % = $(Profit / Actual CP) \times 100 = (180 / 900) \times 100 = 20\%$.
**Answer: 20%**

## 5. Mixtures and Alligations
**Question:** A vessel contains 60 liters of milk and water in the ratio 3:2. How much water must be added to make the ratio 1:1?
**Solution Framework:**
1. Current Milk = $(3/5) \times 60 = 36$ liters. Current Water = $(2/5) \times 60 = 24$ liters.
2. We want the new ratio to be 1:1. This means Milk = Water.
3. Since we are only adding water, the amount of Milk remains constant at 36 liters.
4. Therefore, the new amount of Water must also be 36 liters.
5. Water to add = New Water - Current Water = $36 - 24 = 12$ liters.
**Answer: 12 liters**

## 6. Probability (Coins/Dice)
**Question:** Two dice are thrown simultaneously. What is the probability of getting a sum of 9?
**Solution Framework:**
1. Total possible outcomes when rolling two dice = $6 \times 6 = 36$.
2. Favorable outcomes (sum = 9): (3,6), (4,5), (5,4), (6,3). There are 4 favorable outcomes.
3. Probability = Favorable / Total = $4 / 36 = 1/9$.
**Answer: 1/9**

## 7. Simple and Compound Interest
**Question:** The difference between SI and CI on a certain sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is Rs. 50. Find the principal sum.
**Solution Framework:**
Use the direct formula for the difference between CI and SI for 2 years:
$D = P(R/100)^2$
1. $50 = P(10/100)^2$
2. $50 = P(1/10)^2$
3. $50 = P / 100$
4. $P = 5000$
**Answer: Rs. 5000**

## 8. Permutations and Combinations
**Question:** In how many ways can the letters of the word 'ENGINEERING' be arranged?
**Solution Framework:**
1. Count total letters: 11.
2. Count repeating letters: E (3), N (3), G (2), I (2).
3. Formula = (Total letters!) / (Repetitions!)
4. Arrangements = $11! / (3! \times 3! \times 2! \times 2!)$
*(Calculations are usually not required to full extent in options, expression is enough).*
**Answer: $11! / (3! 3! 2! 2!)$**
